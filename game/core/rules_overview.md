# Chapter 1: Game Overview, Concept & Objectives

---

## 1. Thematic Introduction & High Concept

**Away Team** is a semi-cooperative corporate tactical sci-fi campaign game for **1 to 4 players**.

The solar system has fractured into privatized corporate enclaves. In the shadows of interplanetary conflict, humanity's corporate superpowers fund a joint black-budget peacekeeping initiative known as **The Agency**. Each player takes on the executive role of a **Faction Director**—representing corporate giants such as the kinetic security contractor *Aegis Security Solutions*, the data-monopoly *Nexus Cybernetics*, the aristocratic *Vanderbilt Sovereign Group*, or the subterranean syndicate *Obsidian*.

To neutralize catastrophic existential threats across solar sectors, the Agency deploys a unified **Away Team** of 4 elite specialist operatives into lethal combat zones, derelict research stations, and hostile alien biomes.

## 2. Object of the Game: Victory vs. Task Force Dissolution

Unlike purely cooperative dungeon crawlers or purely competitive skirmish games, **Away Team** forces players to navigate a razor-sharp knife's edge between shared survival and cutthroat corporate greed:

### 2.1 The Collective Threat: Task Force Dissolution
* Every mission has an operational failure limit recorded on the central **Mission Failure Tracker ([FAIL])**.
* If the squad fails to clear a mission's climax stage, is forced to call an emergency retreat, or cannot field a legal 4-operative squad, the tracker advances:
  * **Coalition Loss:** If the Mission Failure Tracker reaches the campaign limit (typically 3 or 4 [FAIL] tokens depending on campaign difficulty), the Agency's funding is terminated. The operation suffers **Task Force Dissolution**—an immediate, catastrophic defeat for all players!

### 2.2 Individual Victory: Corporate Supremacy
* If the campaign concludes with the coalition surviving, the Agency Director who has accumulated the most **Victory Points ([VP])** is declared the winner.
* Victory Points are derived from:
  1. **Liquid Vault Assets:** Unspent Credits ([3 CR] = [1 VP]) and Political Influence ([1 PIP] = [2 VP]).
  2. **Surviving Veteran Operatives:** Mission survival stars on living specialists ([1 LOY] = [2 VP]).
  3. **Dead Pool Martyrdom:** Honor fallen operatives who sacrificed themselves ([1 LOY] = [2 VP]).
  4. **Headquarters Sponsorships:** Permanent corporate-sponsored facilities and landmarks ([2 VP] each).
  5. **Political Agendas & Secret Objectives:** High-value corporate mandates scored throughout the campaign.

## 3. The Semi-Cooperative Paradox & Anti-Quarterbacking

In traditional cooperative games, a single dominant player often "quarterbacks" the table, dictating everyone else's moves. **Away Team** structurally eliminates quarterbacking through the **Semi-Cooperative Paradox**:

1. **Private Vault Sovereignty:** Your Faction's Credits [CR], Effort [EF], and Political Influence [PIP] are stored behind your personal Faction Dashboard. No teammate can force you to spend your Effort to stabilize their wounded agent or burn your static dice in an out-of-focus room.
2. **Individual Operative Dice Sovereignty:** In every stage encounter, each player independently decides how many dice their operative will roll (from 0 dice up to available static stamina). Committing 0 dice ("Holding Back") is completely legal, protecting that operative's finite stamina and avoiding Backfire injury, though it forfeits Momentum refunds.
3. **The Tragedy of the Commons:** Every resource you burn to save a teammate—spending [1 EF] on emergency field medicine, committing finite static dice, or buying medkits—is an asset deducted from your private endgame score. Yet, letting a teammate die advances the mission toward failure and coalition loss.
4. **Binding Promises:** Players may freely negotiate and broker binding economic contracts across the table. You may legally demand [3 CR] in cash from a rival before agreeing to spend your [1 EF] to stabilize their dying operative.

## 4. The 4-Phase Chronological Macro Game Loop

Every round of an **Away Team** campaign progresses through **Four Distinct Phases**:

* **Phase 1: Mission Selection & Intelligence Briefing:** Read the Campaign Dossier, select the next active mission, and preview stage hazards, sector tags, and bounty payouts.
* **Phase 2: Roster Assembly & Command Auction:** Assemble the 4-operative strike team, equip 6-slot anatomical loadouts, and hold the Command Auction to elect the Squad Leader.
* **Phase 3: The Tactical Mission Phase (In the Field):** Progress sequentially through 3 to 5 Stage Cards. Roll static dice pools using the 5-Step Stage Loop (Specialists & Support).
* **Phase 4: Headquarters Downtime Phase (Base Operations):** Return to HQ: Collect payouts, vote Stand-Down, heal wounded in the Infirmary, sponsor facilities, and recruit/procure gear.

## 5. Rulebook Callouts, Examples of Play & Diagram Blueprints

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Golden Rule — Dying Breath Successes Count ([BL-RULES-02]):** During Step 2 (Phase 1 Specialists) or Step 3 (Phase 2 Support), all committed dice resolve simultaneously. If an operative rolls a natural 1 (Backfire) that reduces them to 0 HP alongside successful dice (3+, 4+, or 5+), **all rolled successes on that throw still count toward clearing the Stage**—even if no player stabilizes the operative during Step 4 (resulting in a Martyr KIA).

> [!TIP]
> **Balancing Self-Interest Against Dissolution:** Never let the Mission Failure Tracker reach 1 token away from maximum without establishing clear squad cooperation pacts. Early in a mission, play selfishly to build your vault; late in a mission, coordinate tightly to ensure the coalition survives!

> [!EXAMPLE]
> **Example of Play — The Semi-Cooperative Dilemma:**
> 1. In Stage 3 of a high-difficulty mission, **Aegis** holds the Squad Leader token. The squad is 2 successes short of clearing the room.
> 2. **Titan's** operative *The Juggernaut* is at 1 HP with 2 static dice remaining. Rolling those dice risks rolling a natural 1 Backfire, which would drop The Juggernaut to 0 HP.
> 3. Aegis needs the stage cleared to claim the [+1 PIP] Squad Leader bonus, so Aegis offers a **Binding Promise**: *"If you commit your 2 dice and take Backfire damage, I promise to pay [1 EF] from my vault in Step 4 to stabilize you immediately."*
> 4. Titan agrees! The dice are rolled, the stage is cleared, and Aegis honors the binding contract.
