### 2.1 The 4-Tier Semantic Currency Taxonomy
1. **Primary Spendable Currencies (`[CR]`, `[EF]`, `[PIP]`):**
   * **Credits (`[CR]`):** Liquid capital stored in Faction Vaults. Used for equipment purchases, hiring operatives, facility upgrades, and auction bidding. Fully tradeable.
   * **Effort (`[EF]`):** Tactical operational fuel stored in Faction Vaults. Expended for class abilities, complex gear, logistical refreshes, and triage. Non-tradeable.
   * **Political Influence (`[PIP]`):** Executive leverage. Sponsoring HQ facilities, breaking auction ties, and paying Vulture Taxes. Worth `[2 VP]` each at endgame. Non-tradeable.
2. **Transient In-Mission State (`[MOM]`):**
   * **Operational Momentum (`[MOM]`):** Temporary tactical momentum generated when clearing a stage by $\ge D + 2$ successes. Spent immediately to refund spent dice. Never stored in vaults.
3. **Operative Progression Metric (`[LOY]`):**
   * **Loyalty (`[LOY]`):** Personal mission survival rank bound to individual character cards. Scores `[2 VP]` per point at endgame and unlocks faction perks.
4. **Macro-Campaign Trackers (`[FAIL]`, `[VP]`):**
   * **Mission Failure Tracker (`[FAIL]`):** Global coalition failure counter. Reaching campaign limit triggers Task Force Dissolution.
   * **Victory Points (`[VP]`):** Final corporate score determining the winning Director.

### Step 1: Choose Corporate Factions
Each player selects 1 of the 8 available Faction Dossiers (Aegis, Nexus, Vanderbilt, Obsidian, Titan, Ascendant, Aurelius, or Ironclad) and places its Faction Dashboard in their personal player area.

### Step 2: Initialize Faction Vaults
Take starting currency tokens from the supply bank as printed on your Faction card:
* Standard Starting Vault: **`[5 CR]`**, **`[3 EF]`**, and **`[1 PIP]`**.

### Step 3: Recruit Starting Operative Roster
Each player selects 1 starting Specialist Operative from their Faction's affiliated roster to fill their **Active Deployment Slot**, and places 1 secondary specialist into their **Bench Tray**. Retrieve tactical dice matching your starting operative's Starting Static Pool.

### Step 4: Choose Campaign & Prepare Central Mission Track
Select a Campaign Dossier (*Operation: Iron Dawn* recommended for first-time play). Place the dossier in the center of the table. Set the **Mission Failure Tracker (`[FAIL]`)** marker to **`0`**. Deal the Stage Cards for Mission 1 face-down in a horizontal track. Place the shuffled **Stage Modifier Deck** adjacent.

### Step 5: Establish Agency Headquarters
Place the 5 baseline Facility Boards (*Infirmary*, *Quartermaster*, *Recruitment Bureau*, *Intel Array*, *Boardroom*) in the Central Command Zone. Deal 3 Specialist Operatives face-up into the Recruitment Market, and deal 4 Armory Items face-up into the Quartermaster Shop.

### Step 6: Deal Political Agendas & Secret Objectives
* **Open Agendas:** Deal 3 cards face-up next to the mission track. Any player who fulfills an agenda during the mission claims its bounty immediately.
* **Secret Objectives:** Deal 2 Secret Corporate Objective cards to each player. Each Director privately chooses 1 to keep face-down and discards the other.

## 4. Player Count Variations (The 4-Operative Mandate)

Regardless of player count, every mission enforces a strict squad size of **exactly 4 deployed operatives**:

| Player Count | Operatives Controlled Per Player | Vanguard Slot Handling | Minimum Bench Size |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solo Mode (1P)** | 4 Operatives Controlled | None (Single Director) | 4 Active Operatives |
| **2 Players (2P)** | 2 Operatives Deployed Each | None (Dual-Specialist Front) | 4 Active Operatives (2 per Player) |
| **3 Players (3P)** | 1 Operative Deployed Each | **Slot 4: Vanguard Auction** | 6 Active Operatives (2 per Player) |
| **4 Players (4P)** | 1 Operative Deployed Each | None (Specialist Ownership) | 4 Active Operatives (1 per Player) |

### The 3-Player Vanguard Slot Protocol (`BL-BAL-03`)
In a 3-player game, Slots 1, 2, and 3 are filled by each player's active operative. **Slot 4 is the Unassigned Vanguard Slot**:
1. **The Vanguard Auction:** During Phase 2, players bid `[CR]` to win control of the Vanguard Slot.
2. **Double-Deployment:** The winner deploys a second specialist from their bench into Slot 4.
3. **Strategic Stakes:** While double-deploying drains two dice pools simultaneously, both surviving operatives earn `[+1 LOY]` (`[+2 LOY] = [4 VP]` total), making bidding `[2–3 CR]` mathematically optimal!
