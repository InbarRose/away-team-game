# Tactical Simulator (Standalone Landmark)

* **Sponsorship Cost:** `[2 PIP] + [5 CR]`
* **Sponsorship Reward:** **[+2 PIP]** ([4 VP]) + Faction Token placement
* **Control Mode:** **Sponsor-Controlled** (`Capacity: 1 Resting Operative`)

---

## Landmark Effect

During HQ Downtime, **1 healthy benched operative** assigned to the Tactical Simulator immediately earns **[+1 LOY]** and enters their next mission deployment with **`+2 temporary static dice` on Stage 1**.

Because this Landmark is **Sponsor-Controlled**, the Sponsor Faction chooses which benched operative trains in the simulator each round (or may lease the training slot to a rival Faction).
