# Cryo-Stasis Chamber (Standalone Landmark)

* **Sponsorship Cost:** `2 [PIP] + 6 [CR]`
* **Sponsorship Reward:** **`+2 [PIP]`** (`4 [VP]`) + Faction Token placement
* **Control Mode:** **Sponsor-Controlled** (`Capacity: 1 Operative`)

---

## Landmark Effect

During **Step 3 (Healing & Medical Recovery)** of HQ Downtime, the chamber holds **1 wounded operative** and **automatically restores 100% of their missing Hit Points** without rolling any Healing Dice (completely eliminating the risk of rolling a natural `1` Permanent Trauma).

Because this Landmark is **Sponsor-Controlled**, the Sponsor Faction decides which operative occupies the pod each round—and may openly auction or rent the slot to desperate rival Factions for `[CR]` or items.
