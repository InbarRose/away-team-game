# Roster Retention

* **Ideological Mandate:** Keep your operatives alive and active on the bench, minimizing reliance on disposable conscripts or dead martyrs.
* **Target Metric:** Highest number of **living, active operatives** on your roster, excluding Dead Pool martyrs *(minimum 1 living operative required to qualify)*.

## Tiered Endgame Scoring

| Standing | Reward Payout |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1st Place (Most Living Operatives)** | **[+15 VP]** |
| **2nd Place (2nd Most Living Operatives)** | **[+10 VP]** |
| **3rd Place (3rd Most Living Operatives)** | **[+5 VP]** |

> **Tie Resolution:** Tied Factions each claim the full VP reward for that rank, and the next rank down is skipped.
