# Human Capital Hegemony

* **Ideological Mandate:** Cultivate the most prestigious, highly indoctrinated veteran personnel in the Agency.
* **Target Metric:** Highest collective **Loyalty Points (`[LOY]`)** across your living and dead roster *(minimum 1 `[LOY]` required to qualify)*.

## Tiered Endgame Scoring

| Standing | Reward Payout |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1st Place (Most `[LOY]`)** | **+15 `[VP]`** |
| **2nd Place (2nd Most `[LOY]`)** | **+10 `[VP]`** |
| **3rd Place (3rd Most `[LOY]`)** | **+5 `[VP]`** |

> **Tie Resolution:** Tied Factions each claim the full VP reward for that rank, and the next rank down is skipped.
