# Clean Asset Management

* **Ideological Mandate:** Protect personnel from physical destruction and permanent medical complications.
* **Target Metric:** **Fewest total Permanent Trauma / Critical Injury tokens** across your entire roster *(must possess at least 1 living operative to qualify)*.

## Tiered Endgame Scoring

| Standing | Reward Payout |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1st Place (Fewest Injuries)** | **[+15 VP]** |
| **2nd Place (2nd Fewest Injuries)** | **[+10 VP]** |
| **3rd Place (3rd Fewest Injuries)** | **[+5 VP]** |

> **Tie Resolution:** Tied Factions each claim the full VP reward for that rank, and the next rank down is skipped.
