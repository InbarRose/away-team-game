# Armory Dominance

* **Ideological Mandate:** Hoard the highest volume of military-grade weapons, armor, and prototype hardware.
* **Target Metric:** Highest total **Credit ([CR]) purchase value** of item cards stored in your Faction Vault and equipped on operatives *(minimum [1 CR] of gear required to qualify)*.

## Tiered Endgame Scoring

| Standing | Reward Payout |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1st Place (Most Gear Value)** | **[+15 VP]** |
| **2nd Place (2nd Most Gear Value)** | **[+10 VP]** |
| **3rd Place (3rd Most Gear Value)** | **[+5 VP]** |

> **Tie Resolution:** Tied Factions each claim the full VP reward for that rank, and the next rank down is skipped.
