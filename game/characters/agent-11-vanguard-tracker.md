## Class Ability: Emergency Field Medicine (`1 [EF]`)

Activate immediately after a rolling phase concludes in direct response to damage. **Remove up to `2 Hit Points` lost by a single operative** during this specific stage (once per stage).

---

## Unique Power: Last Stand Protocol (`1.5 ES` — Tier 3 Passive)

The first time per mission Kael is reduced to **exactly `1 HP`**, immediately restore **`+2 spent static dice`** to his active pool and treat all of his rolls for the current and next stage as **Primary Focus (`3+`)**.
