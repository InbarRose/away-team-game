# Agent 05: Kaelen "Echo" Cross (*The Guerrilla*)

* **Class:** Recon (`Primary Combat`)
* **Focus Profile:** Primary: `[Combat]` (`3+`) | Secondary: `[Infiltrate]` (`4+`) | Out of Focus: `5+`
* **Base Stats:** **`3 HP`** (`4.5 ES`) • **`7 Static Dice`** (`3.5 ES`) • **Power:** `1.0 ES` (**Total:** `9.0 ES`)

## Class Ability: Intel Reconnaissance (`1 [EF]`)

Activate during Step 1 (Briefing) to scout the top card of the Stage Modifier deck (or next modular stage) and choose whether to replace the current modifier/stage or purge the scouted card.

---

## Unique Power: Target Acquired (`1.0 ES` — Tier 2 Active)

Once per mission during Step 1 (Briefing) of a **`[Combat]`** or **`[Infiltrate]`** stage, Kaelen can lock onto enemy threat vectors. For the duration of this stage, **all Out-of-Focus Support operatives score successes on a `4+`** (instead of `5+`).
