# Agent 15: Dr. Naomi Kim (*The Virologist*)

* **Class:** Scientist (`Zero-Combat Specialist` — Glass Cannon)
* **Focus Profile:** Primary: `[Intel]` (`3+`) | Secondary: `[Infiltrate]` (`4+`) | Out of Focus: `5+`
* **Base Stats:** **`2 HP`** (`3.0 ES`) • **`7 Static Dice`** (`3.5 ES`) • **Power:** `2.0 ES` • **Class Offset:** `+0.5 ES` (**Total:** `9.0 ES`)

---

## Class Ability: Dual-Vector Mapping (`1 [EF]`)

Activate during Step 1 (Briefing). Choose any Focus category and **add it as a concurrent Operational Focus** for the room, allowing operatives to use either the printed Focus or the added Focus for Phase 1/2 thresholds.

---

## Unique Power: Data Cascade (`2.0 ES` — Tier 3 Active)

Once per mission during Step 1 (Briefing) of an **`[Intel]`** or **`[Infiltrate]`** stage, Naomi may spend **`1 [EF]`** to add **`+4 temporary static dice`** to her pool for that check, with complete immunity to Backfire (`1s`) damage.
