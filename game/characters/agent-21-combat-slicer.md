# Agent 21: Corey "Apex" Miller (*The Combat Slicer*)

* **Class:** Technician
* **Focus Profile:** Primary: `[Combat]` (`3+`) | Secondary: `[Operations]` (`4+`) | Out of Focus: `5+`
* **Base Stats:** **`3 HP`** (`4.5 ES`) • **`7 Static Dice`** (`3.5 ES`) • **Power:** `1.0 ES` • **Class Offset:** `0.0 ES` (**Total:** `9.0 ES`)

---

## Class Ability: Adaptive Calibration (`1 [EF]`)

Activate after rolling dice to **convert any 1 rolled miss into a guaranteed success**, OR activate before rolling to **reduce the `[EF]` activation cost of an equipped `[Complex]` item by `-1 [EF]`** (to a minimum of `0`).

---

## Unique Power: System Overclock (`1.0 ES` — Tier 2 Active)

**Once per stage**, before rolling on an **`[Operations]`** or **`[Combat]`** check, Corey may voluntarily suffer **`1 HP` direct neural feedback damage** (bypassing Armor Buffers) to add **`+3 temporary static dice`** to his roll for this stage.
