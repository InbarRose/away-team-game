# Explosive Fuel Craters

* **Type:** Generic High-Risk Hazard Modifier (`generic_modifier`)
* **Thematic Profile:** Exposed fuel lines or volatile plasma containers littering the floor.

## System Rules

* **Chain Reaction:** The **FIRST natural 1 (Backfire)** rolled by any character during this stage detonates local volatile canisters, dealing **1 point of HP damage to EVERY deployed character** in the room.
* Submitting subsequent natural 1s causes normal single-target Backfire damage.
