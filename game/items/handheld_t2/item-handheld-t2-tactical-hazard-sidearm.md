# Tactical Hazard Sidearm

> *"Breach the airlock, weld the ruptured coolant pipe, and put two rounds into whatever crawled out of the vent."*

## Overview

The **Tactical Hazard Sidearm** is a multi-purpose firearm equipped with underslung environmental engineering tools. It covers the entire physical-industrial triad (`[Combat]`, `[Operations]`, and `[Survival]`), bridging all three domains to `4+` in a single hand slot.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Hand L]` or `[Hand R]` (1 Hand)
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `5 [CR]` *(2.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Focus Coverage:** `[Combat]` / `[Operations]` / `[Survival]`

## Core Mechanics

1. **Tri-Domain Utility (`+1 Temp Die`):** Adds **+1 Temporary Static Die** to your active rolling pool when operating in `[Combat]`, `[Operations]`, or `[Survival]`.
2. **Focus Bridge (`4+`):** Allows an operative who is Out of Focus (`5+`) in any of those three categories to roll them at **Secondary Focus (`4+`)**.
