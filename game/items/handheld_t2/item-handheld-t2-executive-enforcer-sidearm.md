# Executive Enforcer Sidearm

> *"A bespoke instrument of corporate compliance. Reads their pulse, records their confession, and enforces the penalty clause."*

## Overview

The **Executive Enforcer Sidearm** is a specialized interrogation and boardroom security weapon. It bridges and boosts [Combat], [Intel], and [Negotiate], making it a favorite of Internal Affairs officers and corporate fixers.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Hand L] or [Hand R] (1 Hand)
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [5 CR] *(2.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Focus Coverage:** [Combat] / [Intel] / [Negotiate]

## Core Mechanics

1. **Tri-Domain Utility (`+1 Temp Die`):** Adds **+1 Temporary Static Die** to your active rolling pool when operating in [Combat], [Intel], or [Negotiate].
2. **Focus Bridge (`4+`):** Allows an operative who is Out of Focus (`5+`) in any of those three categories to roll them at **Secondary Focus (`4+`)**.
