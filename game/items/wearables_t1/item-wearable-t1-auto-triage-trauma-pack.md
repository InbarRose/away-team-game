# Auto-Triage Trauma Pack

> *"Synced to the squad's biometric telemetry. The instant an operative flatlines toward critical, the med-drone fires a coagulant dart."*

## Overview

The **Auto-Triage Trauma Pack** is a 4-Credit emergency medical safeguard for the entire squad, automatically healing `+1 HP` the first time any squad member drops to `1 HP`.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Pack]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `4 [CR]` *(2.0 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Emergency Rescue (Medical Buffer)

## Core Mechanics

* **Automated Triage:** Once per mission drop, when any character in the squad (including yourself) suffers damage that reduces them to **1 HP**, this pack automatically triggers, restoring **`+1 HP`** to them instantly.
