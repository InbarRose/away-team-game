# Field Logistics Backpack

> *"Extra pouches for stims and breaching charges, plus a complimentary trauma kit courtesy of the Quartermaster."*

## Overview

The **Field Logistics Backpack** expands an operative's consumable capacity by `+2 Consumable Slots` and automatically stocks `1 free Trauma Field Medkit` at the start of every mission drop.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Pack]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `3 [CR]` *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Generic Utility (Inventory Expansion)

## Core Mechanics

* **Expanded Storage:** Increases this character's capacity by **+2 Consumable Slots** (allowing up to 4 simple consumables). Additionally, start every mission drop with **1 free Trauma Field Medkit** loaded into an open consumable slot.
