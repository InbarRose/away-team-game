# Squad Power-Relay Pack

> *"Pulses a wireless induction charge across the squad's exosuits, topping off everyone's reserves before the final room."*

## Overview

The **Squad Power-Relay Pack** provides a once-per-mission squad-wide stamina boost, restoring 1 spent static die to every deployed operative during Step 1 (Briefing).

## Core Mechanics

* **Overcharge Discharge:** Once per mission, during Step 1 (Briefing), activate this pack to immediately restore **1 spent static die** to **every member of the squad**.
