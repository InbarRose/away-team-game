# Adrenaline Surge Harness

> *"Monitors cardiac rhythm. When vital signs hit critical, it dumps fiftyccs of synthetic combat epinephrine straight into the bloodstream."*

## Overview

The **Adrenaline Surge Harness** provides an emergency burst of stamina when an operative is pushed to the brink, restoring 2 spent static dice the first time they drop to 1 HP during a mission.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Body]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `3 [CR]` *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Emergency Tactical Power

## Core Mechanics

* **Second Wind:** The first time this character drops to **1 HP** during a mission, instantly restore **2 spent static dice** back to their active pool.
