# Hazard Insulation Harness

> *"When you have to throw a combat grunt into a reactor repair bay, wrap them in non-conductive weave first."*

## Overview

The **Hazard Insulation Harness** shields operatives when they are forced to roll **Out of Focus (`5+`)**, ignoring the first point of damage they take on any unfamiliar stage.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Body]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [3 CR] *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Out-of-Focus Protection

## Core Mechanics

* **Desperation Cushion:** Ignore the first point of damage (Backfire or stage failure penalty) suffered by this character on any stage where they are **Out of Focus (`5+`)**.
