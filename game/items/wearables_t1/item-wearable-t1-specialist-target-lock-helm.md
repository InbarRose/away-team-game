# Specialist Target-Lock Helm

> *"Calibrated to your primary neural pathways. When you're in your element, nothing slips past the threat-warning perimeter."*

## Overview

The **Specialist Target-Lock Helm** rewards operatives who stick to their **Primary Focus (`3+`)** role, absorbing the first point of Backfire or stage failure damage on every matching stage.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Head]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `3 [CR]` *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Primary Focus Protection

## Core Mechanics

* **Mastery Cushion:** Ignore the first point of damage (Backfire or stage failure penalty) suffered by this character on any stage where they have **Primary Focus (`3+`)**.
