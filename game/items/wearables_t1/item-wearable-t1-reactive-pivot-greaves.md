# Reactive Pivot Greaves

> *"Gyroscopic micro-servos snap your weight sideways the millisecond a proximity sensor trips."*

## Overview

The **Reactive Pivot Greaves** grant a repeatable `4+` saving throw against every point of damage suffered by an operative while working in their **Primary Focus (`3+`)**.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Legs]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [3 CR] *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Primary Focus Saving Throw

## Core Mechanics

* **Defensive Roll:** Whenever this character suffers damage (from Backfire or stage failure penalties) on a stage where they have **Primary Focus (`3+`)**, roll 1 die for free. On a **`4+`**, completely negate that point of damage.
