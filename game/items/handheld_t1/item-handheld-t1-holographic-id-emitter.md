# Holographic ID Emitter

> *"Walk like you own the facility, flash the Level-5 clearance hologram, and let the guards hold the elevator for you."*

## Overview

The **Holographic ID Emitter** is a multi-spectrum credentials spoofer that projects authentic corporate clearances and falsified biometric signatures. It bridges [Infiltrate] and [Negotiate], enabling seamless social engineering and checkpoint infiltration.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Hand L] or [Hand R] (1 Hand)
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [3 CR] *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Focus Coverage:** [Infiltrate] + [Negotiate]

## Core Mechanics

1. **Dual-Domain Assistance (`+1 Temp Die`):** Adds **+1 Temporary Static Die** to your active rolling pool when operating in either [Infiltrate] or [Negotiate].
2. **Focus Bridge (`4+`):** Allows an operative who is Out of Focus (`5+`) in [Infiltrate] or [Negotiate] to roll both categories at **Secondary Focus (`4+`)**.
