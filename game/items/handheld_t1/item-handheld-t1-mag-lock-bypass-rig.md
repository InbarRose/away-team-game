# Mag-Lock Bypass Rig

> *"Why blow the bulkhead with C4 when you can pulse the solenoid and walk through in dead silence?"*

## Overview

The **Mag-Lock Bypass Rig** is a high-frequency electronic lockpicker designed for silent entry and mechanical sabotage. It bridges `[Operations]` and `[Infiltrate]`, making it ideal for bypassing magnetic seals, security doors, and automated turret grids.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Hand L]` or `[Hand R]` (1 Hand)
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `3 [CR]` *(1.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Focus Coverage:** `[Operations]` + `[Infiltrate]`

## Core Mechanics

1. **Dual-Domain Assistance (`+1 Temp Die`):** Adds **+1 Temporary Static Die** to your active rolling pool when operating in either `[Operations]` or `[Infiltrate]`.
2. **Focus Bridge (`4+`):** Allows an operative who is Out of Focus (`5+`) in `[Operations]` or `[Infiltrate]` to roll both categories at **Secondary Focus (`4+`)**.
