# Hazard Tech-Harness

> *"When the ventilation shafts flood with neuro-toxin or plasma coolant, a standard filter buys you ten seconds. A sealed harness buys you the room."*  
> — Ironclad Extraction Safety Manual, Rev. 9

## Overview

The **Hazard Tech-Harness** is a Tier 1 multi-slot rig occupying both the `[Head]` and `[Body]` equipment slots. Engineered for extreme environmental anomaly zones and compromised industrial sectors, it pairs a permanent vitality buffer with an active atmospheric purge system that neutralizes room-wide Stage Modifiers.

---

## Equipment Specifications

* **Slots Required:** `[Head]` + `[Body]` (2 Slots)
* **Market Cost:** `4 [CR]` *(2.0 ES Value)*
* **Tier:** Tier 1 Multi-Slot Rig

---

## Tactical Capabilities

### Passive: Sealed Frame *(Vitality Buffer)*
Gain **+1 Maximum Hit Point** on your character sheet while this rig is equipped.

### Active Cost: Filter Purge *(Spend Die)*
Once per stage, spend **1 static die** from your active pool to ignore all negative Stage Modifier penalties and environmental hazard damage for yourself during that check.
