# Stim-Injector Berserker Rig

> *"Ascendant Bio-Tech calls it 'closed-loop metabolic overdrive.' The field medics who have to scrape the user out of the suit afterward call it 'liquid martyrdom.'"*

## Overview

The **Stim-Injector Berserker Rig** is a high-risk Tier 1 multi-slot combat suit occupying [Body] and [Legs]. It reinforces the wearer's physiology with +1 Maximum HP while hard-wiring sub-dermal stimulant spikes directly into their nervous system—allowing them to burn raw vitality for guaranteed stage successes.

## Tactical Capabilities

### Passive: Adrenaline Shell *(Vitality Buffer)*
Gain **+1 Maximum Hit Point** on your character sheet while this rig is equipped.

### Active Cost: Blood Fuel *(Spend HP)*
Once per stage, suffer **1 point of HP damage** (bypassing armor buffers) to gain **+2 automatic Successes** on your current check.
