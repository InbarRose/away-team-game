# Specialist Apex Cortex Helm

> *"High-density neural dampeners engineered for top-tier operatives working under extreme combat stress."*

## Overview

The **Specialist Apex Cortex Helm** combines and upgrades the protection of both Tier 1 Focus helmets, ignoring up to **2 points of damage per stage** whenever the wearer rolls on either a **Primary (`3+`)** or **Secondary (`4+`)** Focus.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Head]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `7 [CR]` *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Mastery Shield)

## Core Mechanics

* **Mastery Shield:** Ignore up to **2 points of damage** (Backfire or stage failure penalties) suffered by this character per stage whenever rolling on a **Primary (`3+`) or Secondary (`4+`) Focus**.
