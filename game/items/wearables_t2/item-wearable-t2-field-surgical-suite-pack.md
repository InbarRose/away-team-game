# Field Surgical Suite Pack

> *"Compact trauma station equipped with synthetic blood synthesizers and laser cauterizers."*

## Overview

The **Field Surgical Suite Pack** upgrades the Tier 1 Auto-Triage Trauma Pack, automatically restoring **`+2 HP`** the first time any squad member drops to `1 HP` during a mission drop.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Pack]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [8 CR] *(4.0 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Advanced Triage)

## Core Mechanics

* **Advanced Triage:** Once per mission drop, when any character in the squad drops to **1 HP**, this pack automatically triggers, restoring **`+2 HP`** to them instantly.
