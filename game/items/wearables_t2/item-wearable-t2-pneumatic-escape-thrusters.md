# Pneumatic Escape Thrusters

> *"Micro-burst thrusters mounted on the calves for explosive tactical retreats and leap-frogging wounded teammates out of the kill-zone."*

## Overview

The **Pneumatic Escape Thrusters** upgrade the Tier 1 Hydraulic Servo-Struts, granting the wearer complete immunity to stage failure and Tactical Retreat damage while also extending full retreat protection to **1 chosen ally**.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Legs]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [7 CR] *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Squad Disengagement)

## Core Mechanics

* **Squad Disengagement:** Take **0 HP damage** from stage failure penalties and Tactical Retreats. In addition, when your squad declares a Tactical Retreat, choose **1 ally**—they also take 0 damage and suffer no injury roll penalties.
