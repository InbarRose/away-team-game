# Kinetic Overdrive Greaves

> *"High-capacity servo-capacitors that convert high-speed movement into stored kinetic battery power for the next assault."*

## Overview

The **Kinetic Overdrive Greaves** snowball squad success from room to room. Whenever the squad clears a stage with `[MOM]` (`+2` or higher), the wearer gains `+2 Temporary Static Dice` for the following stage.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Legs]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `7 [CR]` *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Tier 2 Exclusive (Momentum Surge)

## Core Mechanics

* **Momentum Surge:** Whenever your squad clears a stage with **Momentum (`+2` or higher)**, gain **`+2` temporary static dice** to your rolling pool during the very next stage.
