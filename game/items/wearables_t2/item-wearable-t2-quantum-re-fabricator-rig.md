# Quantum Re-Fabricator Rig

> *"High-speed molecular printer capable of scanning and replicating squad gear on the fly."*

## Overview

The **Quantum Re-Fabricator Rig** is a Tier 2 Exclusive `[Pack]` that converts cheap Tier 1 consumables into high-value Tier 2 consumables by scanning and duplicating an ally's equipped consumable item mid-mission.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Pack]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `8 [CR]` *(4.0 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Tier 2 Exclusive (Item Duplication)

## Core Mechanics

* **Item Duplication:** Once per drop, during Step 1 (Briefing), destroy 1 consumable in your inventory to immediately scan **1 consumable equipped by an ally** and print an exact duplicate into your open slot.
