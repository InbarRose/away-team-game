# Sub-Dermal Intercept Rig

> *"Directional kinetic projection suit allowing an operative to throw themselves in front of incoming fire without taking a scratch."*

## Overview

The **Sub-Dermal Intercept Rig** is a Tier 2 Exclusive [Body] piece that protects fragile teammates. Once per stage, the wearer can intercept and completely negate 1 point of damage suffered by an adjacent ally.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Body]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [7 CR] *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Tier 2 Exclusive (Damage Intercept)

## Core Mechanics

* **Squad Damage Intercept:** Once per stage, when an adjacent squad member suffers damage, you may step in to take the blow. Negate **1 point of that damage entirely**; neither you nor your ally take damage for that point.
