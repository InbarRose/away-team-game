# Emergency Adrenaline Exoskin

> *"Reinforced body sleeve wired with pressurized epinephrine pumps that fire during vital organ failure."*

## Overview

The **Emergency Adrenaline Exoskin** upgrades the Tier 1 Adrenaline Surge Harness. When the wearer drops to `1 HP`, they not only recover `2 spent static dice` immediately, but also gain `+1 automatic Success` on their next roll.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Body]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [7 CR] *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Second Wind Overdrive)

## Core Mechanics

* **Second Wind Overdrive:** The first time this character drops to **1 HP** during a mission, instantly restore **2 spent static dice** AND gain **`+1` automatic Success** on your next roll.
