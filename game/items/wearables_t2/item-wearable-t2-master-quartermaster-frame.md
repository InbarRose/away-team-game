# Master Quartermaster Frame

> *"Heavy-duty logistical pack frame with automated servo-arms for instant access to field inventory."*

## Overview

The **Master Quartermaster Frame** is the ultimate inventory expansion rig, adding `+3 Consumable Slots` and automatically loading **2 free Trauma Field Medkits** at the start of every mission drop.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Pack]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `8 [CR]` *(4.0 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Deep Storage)

## Core Mechanics

* **Deep Storage:** Increases capacity by **+3 Consumable Slots** AND starts every mission drop with **2 free Trauma Field Medkits** loaded into your open slots.
