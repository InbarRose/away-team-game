# Threat-Predictive Telemetry Helm

> *"Wide-band radar dish and predictive AI that identifies and counteracts facility traps before they trigger."*

## Overview

The **Threat-Predictive Telemetry Helm** provides a zero-Effort countermeasure against brutal Stage Modifiers, allowing the wearer to completely cancel and discard one revealed Stage Modifier card per mission drop.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Head]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `7 [CR]` *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Tier 2 Exclusive (Modifier Nullification)

## Core Mechanics

* **Modifier Nullification:** Once per drop, immediately after the Stage Modifier card for a room is revealed, you may activate this helm to **completely negate and discard** that Stage Modifier for the entire squad.
