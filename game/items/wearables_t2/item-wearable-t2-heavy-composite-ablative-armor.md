# Heavy Composite Ablative Armor

> *"Layered ceramic-steel plates designed to shatter under impact, completely absorbing kinetic blowback."*

## Overview

The **Heavy Composite Ablative Armor** doubles the protection of the Tier 1 ballistic vest, providing a **2 HP damage buffer** per mission drop in a single [Body] slot.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Body]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [7 CR] *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Damage Buffer)

## Core Mechanics

* **Dual Plate Buffer:** Absorbs up to **2 points of HP damage** suffered by this character per mission drop. Place 2 tokens on this card; remove 1 token per point absorbed. Depletes when empty.
