# Orbital Drone Relay Pack

> *"Satellite uplink backpack that launches a micro-recon drone above the operational zone while pulsing an orbital induction recharge."*

## Overview

The **Orbital Drone Relay Pack** upgrades the Tier 1 Squad Power-Relay Pack by combining a squad-wide `+1` static die refresh with immediate reconnaissance of the active room's Stage Modifier card during Step 1 (Briefing).

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** `[Pack]`
* **Quartermaster Cost:** `8 [CR]` *(4.0 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Tier 2 Exclusive (Global Recharge & Scan)

## Core Mechanics

* **Global Recharge & Scan:** Once per drop, during Step 1 (Briefing), activate this pack to restore **1 spent static die to EVERY member of the squad** AND immediately reveal the Stage Modifier card for the room.
