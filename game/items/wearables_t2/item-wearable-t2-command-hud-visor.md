# Command HUD Visor

> *"Advanced battle-network visor that continuously recalculates optimal firing lines and hazard vectors across the entire room."*

## Overview

The **Command HUD Visor** is the Tier 2 upgrade to the classic Tactical HUD Visor, doubling its dice-correction power by allowing the wearer to re-roll up to **2 failure faces** (blanks or natural 1s) on any check once per stage.

## Equipment Profile

* **Slot Requirement:** [Head]
* **Quartermaster Cost:** [7 CR] *(3.5 ES Value)*
* **Deck Quantity:** 1 Copy
* **Equipment Type:** Upgraded Classic (Enhanced Targeting)

## Core Mechanics

* **Enhanced Targeting:** Once per stage, after your initial roll, you may re-roll up to **2 failure faces** (blanks or `1`s) on any check.
