# **AWAY TEAM — ECONOMY SYSTEM DESIGN & BASELINE METAGAME**

This document establishes the mathematical baseline, economy metrics, and non-card static systems for *Away Team* version 1.0. Use this framework to anchor all subsequent card designs, item costs, and mission difficulties to ensure perfect structural balance.

## **📊 PART 1: THE METAGAME ECONOMY & UNIT OF POWER**

To ensure asymmetrical faction powers and randomized items don't break the system, all components are balanced against a centralized mathematical anchor: the **Value of a Success**.

### **1.1 The Mathematical Anchor: The \$d6\$ Probability Matrix**

Because dice pools are static and non-refreshing, every die rolled represents an expenditure of a finite operational resource.  
The mathematical expected value (\$E\$) of successes generated by a single committed die roll varies across the Skewed Pyramid:

* **Specialist Roll (3+):** Succeeds on a 3, 4, 5, or 6\.  
* \$\$E\[\\text{Specialist}\] \= \\frac{4}{6} \\approx 0.67 \\text{ successes per die}\$\$  
* **Matched Support (4+):** Succeeds on a 4, 5, or 6\.  
* \$\$E\[\\text{Matched}\] \= \\frac{3}{6} \= 0.50 \\text{ successes per die}\$\$  
* **Unmatched Support (5+):** Succeeds on a 5 or 6\.  
* \$\$E\[\\text{Unmatched}\] \= \\frac{2}{6} \\approx 0.33 \\text{ successes per die}\$\$  
* **The Baseline Risk:** Every single die thrown faces a flat \$\\frac{1}{6}\$ (\$\\approx 16.7\\%\$) chance of rolling a natural 1, triggering a **Backfire** hazard.

### **1.2 The Universal Conversion Scale**

To evaluate the power level of abilities, items, and card rewards, everything is converted into a standard baseline value: **1 Power Unit (PU) \= 1 Guaranteed Success**.  
Using this anchor, the core currencies convert across the design space as follows:

| Component Currency | Baseline Power Unit (PU) Value | Economic Justification & Balance Metric |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1 Success** | **1.00 PU** | The fundamental currency required to clear stage difficulties. |
| **1 Specialist Die** | **0.67 PU** | Based on the 3+ success probability matrix. |
| **1 Unmatched Die** | **0.33 PU** | Based on the 5+ success probability matrix. |
| **1 Universal Token** | **1.33 PU** | Standardized fuel cost. A token typically buys \$+2\$ Specialist dice, generating an expected value of \$1.33\$ successes. |
| **1 Faction Credit** | **1.00 PU** | Tied directly to base building. In the HQ phase, **1 Credit \= 1 guaranteed VP**, locking its baseline value to the core score track. |
| **1 Political Influence (PIP)** | **2.00 PU** | High scarcity leverage. Equal to the value of absorbing a 2-item corpse drop or forcing an infrastructure veto. |
| **1 Victory Point (VP)** | **1.00 PU** | The final endgame metric. Balanced tightly against the liquid Credit economy. |

### **1.3 Stage Difficulty Balancing Formulas**

When designing the linear Stage tracks for campaigns, the system uses the player count (\$N\$) to calculate baseline target difficulties for rooms:

* **Standard Stage Difficulty:** Calculated as \$\\text{Difficulty} \= N \+ 2\$. A 3-player game features a standard baseline stage difficulty of 5\.  
* **Elite Stage Difficulty:** Calculated as \$\\text{Difficulty} \= (N \\times 2\) \+ 1\$. A 3-player game features an Elite threat tier of 7 successes.  
* **The Campaign Attrition Budget:** A standard 3-stage mission expects a 4-agent squad to face a collective difficulty total of 14 to 16 successes. Because a 4-agent squad drops with a baseline of roughly 16 physical dice total, players must utilize Specialists or spend Tokens/Items to avoid complete dice exhaustion before the final room.

## **🪖 PART 3: STATIC SYSTEM DESIGN — THE 4 CORE CLASSES**

To organize the 24 unique character cards across the Skewed Tag Pyramid, personnel are divided into 4 core functional disciplines. Every character has an integrated **Class Active Ability** with fixed token costs that remain unchanged across all variations.

### **3.1 Soldier Class**

* *Tag Alignment:* Heavy Primary \[Combat\], Secondary variance in \[Survival\] or \[Infiltrate\].  
* **Class Ability — Lock and Load (Cost: 1 Faction Token):** Activate during the Phase 1 Initiative step of a \[Combat\] stage. Add **\+2 physical dice** from the box directly to this character's active pool for this roll. These bonus dice are returned to the box after the roll and do not drain the character's static mission pool.

### **3.2 Recon Class**

* *Tag Alignment:* Heavy Primary \[Infiltrate\], Secondary variance in \[Intel\] or \[Combat\].  
* **Class Ability — Evasion Vectors (Cost: 1 Faction Token):** Activate immediately after a rolling phase concludes on an \[Infiltrate\] or \[Operations\] stage. You may completely negate up to **2 points of physical Backfire damage** generated by natural 1s thrown by any members of the active squad.

### **3.3 Scientist Class**

* *Tag Alignment:* Heavy Primary \[Intel\], Secondary variance in \[Survival\] or \[Operations\].  
* **Class Ability — Field Medicine (Cost: 2 Faction Tokens):** Activate exclusively during the Transition Step between stage cards or within the HQ Phase. Choose any single character on the active deployment squad or home roster bench and **heal 1 missing Hit Point**.

### **3.4 Diplomat Class**

* *Tag Alignment:* Heavy Primary \[Negotiate\], Secondary variance in \[Operations\] or \[Intel\].  
* **Class Ability — Bureaucratic Override (Cost: 1 Faction Token):** Activate during a \[Negotiate\] or \[Intel\] Stage Briefing before any dice are committed. Change the baseline success threshold for all Matched and Unmatched support characters in the room to a flat **4+** for the duration of the stage.

## **🏢 PART 4: STATIC SYSTEM DESIGN — HQ FACILITIES MATRIX**

The Global Infrastructure Board contains 3 primary layout tracks, each branching into 3 progressive technological tiers. These structures require collective credit funding during the HQ Phase.

### **4.1 The Medical Wing Branch**

Focuses on mitigating the Home Base Injury Cycle, reversing severe trauma, and keeping veteran character assets in active deployment status.

* **Tier 1: Trauma Ward (Cost: 6 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+3 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* During the Aftermath Phase, the team may collectively trigger **1 reroll per phase** on the Home Base Injury Chart to override a permanent injury result (a natural 1 or 2).  
* **Tier 2: Rehab Lab (Cost: 10 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+3 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* During the HQ downtime phase, any Faction may spend **3 universal Tokens** from their vault to completely wipe away a *Permanent Injury* scar trait from a benched veteran.  
* **Tier 3: Cybernetic Lab (Cost: 16 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+4 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* Any Faction may pay a flat fee of **5 Credits** to purge all injury scars from a living agent, replacing their flesh with cybernetic parts that grant permanent immunity to all future \[Operations\] or \[Survival\] stage damage penalties.

### **4.2 The Armory Vault Branch**

Focuses on weapon modifications, logistical item deployment, and weaponizing characters before they launch.

* **Tier 1: Mod Assembly (Cost: 8 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+3 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* Unlocks the Weapon Modification slot on character sheets. Factions can pay 1 Credit during loadout to attach a modification card. Once per mission drop, this modification allows a player to flip a single failure face into a **guaranteed success**.  
* **Tier 2: Supply Network (Cost: 12 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+3 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* Once per mission drop, during any active Stage Briefing, any deployed character can spend **2 universal Tokens** from their current pool to immediately draw a random Consumable item card directly from the base storage deck into common squad inventory.  
* **Tier 3: Prototype Foundry (Cost: 18 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+4 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* Unlocks top-tier prototype weapon profiles in the Shared Armory market. Furthermore, master modifications can now be fabricated for 3 Credits. A master modification allows an agent to treat a rolled natural 1 as a standard, bloodless failure face instead of taking Backfire damage once per stage.

### **4.3 The Training Pavilion Branch**

Focuses on tactical flexibility, attribute advancement, and increasing resource storage limits.

* **Tier 1: Cross-Training (Cost: 8 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+3 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* Deployed characters can pay a **2 Credit fee** during the pre-launch loadout step to temporarily append a new **Secondary Label** to their active sheet for the duration of the drop, shifting their success matrix from a 5+ to a 4+ in that category.  
* **Tier 2: Peak Conditioning (Cost: 14 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+3 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* When a character crosses into Loyalty Rank 3 or Rank 6, their owning Faction can immediately pay 3 Credits to permanently increase that specific agent's maximum health pool cap by **\+1 HP** or permanently add **\+1 die** to their baseline static mission pool.  
* **Tier 3: Agency Headquarters (Cost: 20 Credits \| Lead Architect: \+5 VPs)**  
  * *Team Passive Benefit:* The absolute ceiling for universal tokens stored on individual Faction Dashboard tracks is permanently raised from a maximum cap of 5 tokens up to a maximum cap of **7 tokens**.

\# SECTION 4.4: OPERATIONAL COMMAND (THE SQUAD LEADER)  
Before the drop ship leaves the hangar during the Loadout Step, players must determine who holds absolute operational authority for the incoming drop. This is resolved via the \*\*Command Auction\*\*.  
\#\# 🔨 THE COMMAND AUCTION  
Once the 4-agent squad is assembled, players bid for the right to claim the \*\*Squad Leader\*\* title token for their deployed agent.  
 \* \*\*The Currency:\*\* Bidding is conducted out-of-hand using \*\*Credits\*\* from Faction Vaults.  
 \* \*\*The Resolution:\*\* The highest bidder drops the promised cash into the central supply bank and clips the \*Squad Leader Token\* to their character card. (In the case of a tie, the player with the highest current \*\*PIP\*\* wins. If still tied, the player closest to the first player token wins).  
\#\# 🏛️ EXECUTIVE OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY  
During the mission, the player controlling the Squad Leader holds absolute final veto and decision-making power over all non-rolling operational choices. This includes:  
 \* Choosing which branching path to take on the Operations Track.  
 \* Deciding how to resolve narrative choices or dilemmas on Stage Cards.  
 \* Dictating whether the squad will vote to \*Concede a Stage\* or push forward.  
 \* Declaring a formal \*\*Mission Retreat\*\*.  
\#\# 🎲 COMMAND ABILITY: LOGISTICAL RESUPPLY  
The Agency grants the active Squad Leader a direct communication link to orbital supply drops. The Squad Leader may spend their Faction's pooled \*\*Effort\*\* directly from their vault to inject fresh dice back into the squad's fading static pools.  
 \* \*\*Logistical Refresh (Tier 1):\*\* Spend \*\*2 Effort\*\* to immediately restore \*\*1 missing die\*\* to \*every\* deployed character’s active static pool.  
 \* \*\*Logistical Refresh (Tier 2):\*\* Spend \*\*3 Effort\*\* to immediately restore \*\*2 missing dice\*\* to \*every\* deployed character’s active static pool.  
 \* \*\*Timing Window:\*\* This ability can only be triggered during the \*Transition Step\* between rooms. It cannot be used mid-firefight to alter an active rolling phase.  
\#\# 🥾 THE PROTOCOLS OF RETREAT  
When an operation turns into an absolute meat-grinder, the Squad Leader can make the ultimate executive command to abort the mission, prioritizing personnel preservation over corporate objectives. A retreat automatically marks the mission as a \*\*Failure\*\* on the campaign tracker, but saves the squad from permanent trauma.  
\#\#\# 1\. Tactical Retreat (The Controlled Abort)  
The Squad Leader may voluntarily call a Tactical Retreat during any Transition Step, provided the following operational conditions are met:  
 \* The squad has successfully cleared \*\*at least the first stage\*\* of the mission.  
 \* \*\*AND\*\* either any single character card is currently sitting at exactly \*\*1 Hit Point\*\*, \*\*OR\*\* more than \*\*half (50%)\*\* of all collective starting dice across the entire squad have been permanently spent.  
\#\#\# 2\. Forced Retreat (The Emergency Extraction)  
If the squad completely fails to generate enough successes to clear a room and suffers a \*\*Stage Failure Penalty\*\*, the structural integrity of the drop breaks. The Squad Leader can immediately choose to trigger a Forced Retreat, bypassing the remaining rooms on the track and pulling the team out alive.  
\#\# 📊 ACCOUNTABILITY: RISK & REWARD STAKES  
Commanding an operation yields immense corporate leverage, but failure is met with severe institutional demotion on the executive floor.  
\| Mission Resolution Outcome \| Faction Political Impact \| Personnel Career Impact \|  
\|---\|---\|---\|  
\| 🎉 \*\*Successful Mission Clear\*\* \| \*\*+1 PIP\*\* to the Faction Vault \| \*\*+1 Loyalty Rank\*\* to the Squad Leader character card. \|  
\| 💀 \*\*Mission Failure / Abortion\*\* \| \*\*-1 PIP\*\* from the Faction Vault \| \*\*0 Loyalty\*\* gained. Character rolls on the Injury Chart normally. \|  
\#\# ⚖️ Why the Math Here is Genuinely Brilliant  
Your dice-restoration cost structure matches our underlying \*\*Universal Conversion Scale\*\* flawlessly:  
 \* \*\*2 Effort for 1 Die Per Character (4 Dice Total):\*\* 2 Effort costs your vault a value of 2.66 Power Units. In return, you get 4 total dice across the squad. If rolled by Specialists/Matched support, those 4 dice possess an expected mathematical return of \*\*2.00 to 2.68 guaranteed successes\*\*. The investment is perfectly tight.  
 \* \*\*3 Effort for 2 Dice Per Character (8 Dice Total):\*\* 3 Effort costs your vault a value of 4.00 Power Units. In return, you dump a massive chunk of 8 dice back into play. 8 dice rolling across primary/secondary lines generate an expected return of exactly \*\*4.00 successes\*\*.  
It is a beautiful mechanical valve. It gives players a high-efficiency way to save a failing run, but the steep Effort cost directly starves their vault, leaving them with fewer tokens to run their personal Class Abilities in the next room.

\#\# 📐 PART 1: THE RECALIBRATED MISSION ECONOMY

With characters bringing \*\*5 to 8 dice\*\* each and missions spanning \*\*3 to 5 stages\*\*, dice management becomes a high-stakes tactical puzzle.

\* \*\*Total Squad Pool (4 Agents):\*\* 20 to 32 dice total for the entire mission.  
\* \*\*Stage Difficulty Curve:\*\* Escalates linearly across the drop:  
\* \*Stage 1 (Entry):\* \*\*2–3 Successes\*\* \*(Light guard post / entry hazard)\*  
\* \*Stage 2 (Infiltration):\* \*\*3 Successes\*\* \*(Internal security / patrol)\*  
\* \*Stage 3 (Core Objective):\* \*\*4 Successes\*\* \*(Mainframe breach / vault locks)\*  
\* \*Stage 4 (Climax / Extraction):\* \*\*4–5 Successes\*\* \*(Heavy response force / lockdown)\*

\* \*\*Total Required Successes:\*\* \~12 to 15 successes per mission.  
\* \*\*The Pressure Valve:\*\* Because 24 dice rolling against a mix of 3+, 4+, and 5+ thresholds will naturally burn through dice quickly, players \*\*cannot\*\* roll maximum dice every room. The Squad Leader's \*Logistical Refresh\* (spending Effort to restore dice) and tactical choices to lower stage difficulties become essential to survival.

This is a brilliant mechanical tweak. It directly solves one of the biggest behavioral traps in static-pool dice games: **the conservative hoarding paralysis.**  
When dice pools are finite, players naturally default to "penny-pinching"—rolling the bare minimum to scrape by, which leads to slow, risk-averse play and frequent tragic wipes when dice whiff.  
By letting **Momentum scale per success starting at \+2**, you turn over-committing from a "waste of resources" into a **high-reward gambit**. Players are actively incentivized to flood the table with dice, hit over-kill thresholds, and recycle their ammo back into the squad.

## **⚡ RULEBOOK PROSE: OPERATIONAL MOMENTUM**

### **1\. Generating Momentum**

When a squad clears a stage and generates successes beyond the stage's required Difficulty (\$D\$), any excess successes starting at **\+2 above Target** generate **Momentum**:  
\$\$\\text{Momentum} \= \\text{Total Successes} \- \\text{Difficulty} \- 1 \\quad (\\text{for Total Successes} \\ge D \+ 2)\$\$

| Target Difficulty (D) | Total Successes Rolled | Margin of Victory | Momentum Awarded |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **3** | 3 | \+0 | **0** (Stage Cleared) |
| **3** | 4 | \+1 | **0** (Clean Victory) |
| **3** | 5 | **\+2** | **1 Momentum** |
| **3** | 6 | **\+3** | **2 Momentum** |
| **3** | 7 | **\+4** | **3 Momentum** |

### **2\. Spending Momentum (Squad Leader Command)**

Immediately during Step 5 (Resolution) of the completed stage, the **Squad Leader** distributes all generated Momentum tokens to the squad.

* **The Exchange Rate:** **1 Momentum \= Restore 1 spent die** directly back to an agent's active static pool.  
* **The Participant Constraint:** Momentum dice can **ONLY** be restored to characters who actually committed and rolled at least 1 die during the stage just completed.

💡 **Operational Design Note:** Benchwarmers who held back to save their strength cannot leech Momentum. To get your ammo back, you have to put skin in the game.

## **🧠 WHY THIS GAME-DESIGN LOGIC WORKS**

1. **Eliminates "Feel-Bad" Over-Rolls:** In most games, rolling 6 successes on a 3-difficulty check feels like "wasted luck." Here, rolling 6 successes means you cleared the room *and* refund 2 dice back to the strike team.  
2. **Encourages Specialist Aggression:** A Specialist with a Primary Focus (striking on 3+) will want to dump 4 or 5 dice into an easy stage because their high success probability makes hitting the \+2 Momentum threshold a reliable dice-refueling engine.  
3. **Elevates Squad Leader Dynamics:** The Squad Leader gets to play the hero at the end of a hard-won stage, handing out refunded dice like fresh magazines to the frontline operatives who just put in the work.

## **🔨 7.2 AUCTION PROTOCOLS & TABLE POLITICS**

### **1\. The Stand-Down Auction (Mission vs. R\&R)**

Before committing to a new deployment, players must determine if the Agency enters a maintenance break ("Healing Round") or pushes directly into the next operation.

* **Minimum Bid:** **0 Credits** paid directly from a Faction Vault to the HQ bank.  
* **Negotiation:** Players may trade Credits, items, or promises out-of-hand to sway bids.  
* **Tie-Breakers:**  
  * Highest Credit Bid  
  * Highest Faction PIP  
  * Highest Character Loyalty (highest single agent on roster)  
  * Die Roll-Off  
* **The Decision:** The winning bidder unilaterally selects one outcome:  
  * **DEPLOY:** Skip directly to medical resolution and prepare for the next Mission Briefing.  
  * **STAND-DOWN:** Skip the mission drop entirely. The Agency enters an extended maintenance round:  
    * Infirmary capacity increases by **\+2 Treatment Beds** (4 total beds).

# **SYSTEM SPECIFICATION: CAMPAIGN, MISSION, & STAGE ARCHITECTURE**

## **🏛️ SECTION 1: CAMPAIGN LOGIC**

### **1\. Structure & Victory Condition**

* **Sequence:** A campaign consists of **3 to 5 Campaign Missions** arranged in a fixed, story-driven linear sequence.  
* **Win Condition:** The squad wins the campaign by successfully completing all Campaign Missions in order.  
* **Side-Missions:** Optional operations available between Campaign Missions that allow players to earn extra Credits, items, Effort, or Loyalty.

### **2\. The Doom Tracker & Game Over**

* **The Doom Tracker:** A master track counting down operational collapse. Reaching the maximum Doom threshold results in immediate **Game Over** (Agency Collapse).  
* **Doom Triggers:** Added when a Campaign Mission fails, or as dictated by specific campaign difficulty rules (e.g., failing a high-stakes side-mission).

### **3\. Campaign Difficulty Levers (Configured by Campaign Card)**

Each campaign dossier sets its own difficulty parameters:

* **Mission Count:** 3 (Simple/Standard) up to 5 (Complex/Hard).  
* **Max Doom Threshold:** Typically 3 to 5 Doom Points.  
* **Side-Mission Limits:** Unlimited access, capped count (e.g., max 2 side-missions total), or lockout windows (e.g., side-missions locked after Mission 3).  
* **Side-Mission Failure Penalties:** Whether failing a side-mission adds Doom Points or carries only local tactical consequences.  
* **Retry Rules:** Whether failed Campaign Missions can be retried freely, retried with increased difficulty (e.g., heightened alert levels), or cause permanent branching penalties.

## **📜 SECTION 2: MISSION LOGIC**

### **1\. Narrative Arc & Preset Stages**

* **Preset Composition:** Missions are not built from random stage draws. Each mission features a fixed, curated sequence of stages designed to tell a specific tactical story.  
* **Mandatory Combat Rule:** Every mission (Campaign or Side-Mission) **must contain at least 1** \[Combat\] **stage**.

### **2\. Mission Resolution**

* **Mission Clearance:** Achieved when the squad clears the final climax stage of the mission.  
* **Mission Failure:** Triggered when the squad fails a critical/climax stage or declares an emergency retreat. Mission failures escalate campaign-level consequences (e.g., adding Doom Points or altering future campaign states).

## **🎯 SECTION 3: STAGE LOGIC & THE FOCUS PYRAMID**

### **1\. The 50% Focus Pyramid Rule**

Across the entire game system, at least **50% of all stages** feature the \[Combat\] Focus.

### **2\. Stage Failure Penalties (Tactical Blowback)**

Failing an individual stage inflicts immediate tactical penalties on the squad. **Stage failures do not directly add Doom Points**; Doom is handled strictly at the Mission/Campaign level. Stage failure penalties include:

* Direct physical HP damage or Trauma/Critical Injuries to operatives.  
* Loss of equipped items, Credits, or Faction Vault Effort.  
* Downstream Target Difficulty increases on upcoming stages in the same mission.

### **3\. Dynamic Focus Swaps (Squad Leader Options)**

During Step 1 (Briefing) of a stage, the Squad Leader may declare a **Dynamic Focus Swap** to alter how the room is executed:

* 💥 **Force Breach (**\[Non-Combat\] **➔** \[Combat\]**):** Turn a technical, stealth, or social stage into a brute-force firefight. The stage becomes \[Combat\], but Target Difficulty increases by **\+1 or \+2 Successes** due to defensive alarms.  
* 🌿 **Tactical Bypass (**\[Combat\] **➔** \[Non-Combat\]**):** Detour around a violent patrol by hacking security gates (\[Operations\]) or navigating dangerous terrain (\[Survival\]). Requires spending a specific resource (e.g., 1 Effort or an equipped item).

## **🃏 SECTION 4: STAGE MODIFIER CARDS**

### **1\. Overview**

While stages are fixed and narrative, every stage draws 1 **Stage Modifier Card** during Step 1 (Briefing). Modifiers inject field hazards, technical twists, and tactical opportunities into the preset room.

### **2\. Universal Modifier Mechanics**

Stage Modifier Cards are designed to function on any stage regardless of printed Focus, utilizing two design approaches:

* **Split-Effect Modifiers:** The card applies distinct text based on the room's active Focus category:  
  * *If* \[Combat\] *Focus:* Enemies carry heavy body armor; re-rolls cost 1 extra die.  
  * *If* \[Intel\] */* \[Operations\] *Focus:* Terminals are locked behind ICE; failure faces cause Effort drain.  
  * *If Other (*\[Infiltrate\]*,* \[Survival\]*,* \[Negotiate\]*):* Motion sensors are active; natural 1s inflict double damage.  
* **Generic System Modifiers:** Focus-agnostic environment or rule shifts:  
  * *Difficulty Shifts:* Increase or decrease Target Difficulty by \$\\pm 1\$.  
  * *Bounty / Penalty Shifts:* Grants bonus Credit/Effort payouts on success, or inflicts extra item/stat loss on stage failure.  
  * *Random Squad Hazard:* All deployed operatives roll 1 die during Briefing for a random chance at minor damage, resource loss, or temporary tactical buffs.

# **🌐 CAMPAIGN NARRATIVE & RETRY LOGIC**

## **1\. Macro-Spacing (Time & Space)**

* **Separation:** Campaign Missions take place in different locations, facilities, or planets, with days or weeks of in-world prep time between drops.  
* **The Return to HQ:** The squad returning to HQ between missions represents completing a major strategic leg of the campaign. They return to process intelligence, heal trauma, trade gear, and prepare the next strike.

## **2\. Coherent Retry Framing (No Narrative Paradoxes)**

* **Objective Framing:** Campaign Missions are framed around **achieving an operational goal** (e.g., *Sabotage the Relay*, *Extract the VIP*, *Raid the Supply Depot*) rather than situational escape traps (e.g., *Escape the collapsing room*).  
* **The Retry Logic:** If a Campaign Mission fails, the story narrative states that the squad was forced into a **Tactical Retreat** before completing the main objective. Retrying the mission means launching a **Second Assault Wave** on that same location, with the enemy now aware of the threat.

## **3\. Hard Mode Variant: "Back-to-Back Drops" (**\[No HQ\]**)**

* **Advanced Mechanics:** Certain hard/complex campaigns feature the \[Continuous Drop\] tag on late-game missions (e.g., Mission 4 ➔ Mission 5).  
* **Rules Impact:** Upon clearing Mission 4, the squad **cannot return to HQ**. They skip the HQ Downtime Phase entirely, retain their remaining dice/HP/consumables, and deploy immediately into Mission 5\.

## **🏷️ SECTION 1: THEME TAG SYSTEM**

To ensure absolute narrative immersion, every Side-Mission card features 1 to 3 **Environment & Context Themes**. A Campaign Dossier specifies its **Allowed Themes**, dictating which Side-Missions are seeded into the available market during HQ Downtime.

### **Core Theme Registry**

* 🏙️ \[Urban\]**:** Corporate arcologies, neon city districts, underworld black markets, transport hubs.  
* 🛰️ \[Space\]**:** Orbital stations, derelict starships, zero-g satellites, high-orbit arrays.  
* 🌲 \[Planet\]**:** Wilderness incursions, alien biomes, surface outposts, mountain passes.  
* 🔬 \[Facility\]**:** Subterranean labs, automated factories, containment sectors, bunker complexes.  
* 💼 \[Covert\]**:** Executive floors, high-society galas, data centers, political safehouses.

## **🛠️ SECTION 2: RESOLVING THE "FACILITY / BASE" HQ PARADOX**

When an entire campaign takes place inside a single monolithic mega-facility or enclosed complex, returning to "Global HQ" after every drop breaks immersion.  
To solve this, campaigns with the \[Facility\] theme activate **Safehouse Downtime Protocol**:

### **1\. The Operational Safehouse (Sub-HQ)**

* **Narrative Reframing:** Returning to HQ is re-flavored as retreating to a **Secured Sector / Hidden Vent Hub / Sub-Level Safehouse** inside the complex.  
* **Infirmary & Rest:** Healing and injury recovery take place using field-expedient med-bays, portable stasis pods, or stim-treatments inside the safehouse.

### **2\. Siphon Procurement (Quartermaster In-Situ)**

Instead of walking into a central warehouse, players access equipment during Downtime via:

* **Terminal Hacks:** Printing gear via local industrial 3D fabricators.  
* **Supply Pod Drops:** Orbital supply pods punched through ceiling ventilation.  
* **Black-Market Splicers:** Local rogue engineers operating inside the maintenance tunnels.

## **🛏️ 1\. MED BAY CAPACITY & HEALING ROLLS**

When the squad returns to base, unhealed damage must be treated in the **Infirmary** during HQ Downtime.

* **Bed Limits:** Baseline HQ provides **2 Treatment Beds**. Operatives who cannot fit in a bed cannot roll to heal during that Downtime.  
* **Med Bay Dice Pool:** Each bed grants **3 Healing Dice** (or **5 Healing Dice** during a *Stand-Down* round).  
* **Base Threshold:** Healing dice succeed on a **5+** (lowered to **4+** with the *Trauma Cybernetics* HQ upgrade).

### **Resolving Healing Rolls**

For each bed, assign the Med Bay's Healing Dice to the operative's missing HP points and roll:

* **Success (5+ / 4+ upgraded):** Clears **1 point of damage** cleanly.  
* **Natural 1 (Calcification/Complication):** That specific point of damage becomes a **Permanent Trauma**.  
* **Failure (Blank/Miss):** The damage point is not healed this round.

## **🩹 2\. CRITICAL INJURIES VS. PERMANENT TRAUMA**

### **⚠️ Critical Injury Token (Temporary / Unhealed Damage)**

Any point of damage that is **not healed** by the end of Downtime (due to lack of beds, insufficient dice, or failed rolls) converts into a **Critical Injury Token**.

* **Deployment Penalty:** Each Critical Injury Token inflicts **\-1 Maximum HP** AND **\-1 Starting Static Die** at the launch of a mission.  
* **Deployability:** Operatives carrying Critical Injuries **can still deploy on missions**, but they enter the field severely weakened.  
* **Future Healing (-1 Penalty):** During subsequent Downtimes, an operative with Critical Injury Tokens can be assigned to a Med Bay bed again. However, because the wound is old, healing rolls suffer a **\-1 penalty** (e.g., requiring a **6+**, or **5+** with *Trauma Cybernetics*).

### **🔒 Permanent Trauma Token (Locked Complication)**

If a Med Bay Healing Die rolls a **natural 1**, that point of damage calcifies into a **Permanent Trauma Token**.

* **Deployment Penalty:** Functions like a Critical Injury (**\-1 Maximum HP**, **\-1 Starting Static Die**)\[cite: 1, 2\].  
* **Locked Status:** Permanent Trauma **cannot be attempted to heal again** through standard Med Bay rolls or normal Downtimes\[cite: 1\].  
* **Higher-Tier Resolution:** Can only be purged by high-tier HQ technology (like the *Cybernetic Lab*)\[cite: 1\].

## **📜 CORE TIERED SCORING RULES**

### **1\. The Standard Tier Matrix**

When hidden playbooks are revealed during the final campaign audit, players compare their standings in the card's target metric:

* 🥇 **1st Place (Top Tier):** **\+15 Victory Points (VPs)**  
* 🥈 **2nd Place (Mid Tier):** **\+10 Victory Points (VPs)**  
* 🥉 **3rd Place (Base Tier):** **\+5 Victory Points (VPs)**

### **2\. The Baseline Qualification Rule**

To earn any VP payout (even 3rd Place), a Faction must possess **at least 1 unit** in the target metric. You cannot claim 5 VPs for 3rd place if you have 0 in that category.

### **3\. Tie Resolution**

If two or more Factions tie for a rank, both Factions claim the full VP reward for that rank, and the next rank down is skipped. *(Example: Two players tie for 1st place \$\\rightarrow\$ Both earn 15 VPs; the next player takes 3rd place for 5 VPs).*

# **🃏 SYSTEM SPECIFICATION: THE 12 THEMATIC MULTI-TIER PERK CARDS**

## **📜 MULTI-TIER PERK CARD ARCHITECTURE**

* **Modular Doctrines:** Instead of single-tier cards, each Perk Card represents a complete **Thematic Tactical Doctrine**.  
*   
* **Tier Progression:** Cards feature abilities mapped to **Loyalty Ranks (Tier 1: 1 Loy \| Tier 2: 3 Loy \| Tier 3: 6 Loy \| Tier 4: 10 Loy)**. As operatives or Factions advance in Loyalty, higher tiers on equipped cards unlock automatically.  
*   
* **Structural Distribution:** Each card contains at least **Tier 1 and one higher tier** (most feature 3 tiers, select cards feature 2 or 4). Abilities have explicit activation costs (Credits, Effort, HP, PIP, or operational triggers).  
*
